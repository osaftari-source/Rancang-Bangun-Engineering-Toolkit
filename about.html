Purpose & Development Status

Purpose, active modules, candidate development roadmap, and update status of this internal engineering initiative.

Developer Message

Rancang Bangun Engineering Toolkit is developed to help engineers perform quick preliminary calculations, early evaluations, and site-related assessments more efficiently.

This toolkit supports engineering work; it does not replace approved engineering calculations, applicable codes, project specifications, vendor documents, or professional engineering review.

Current Development Phase

Phase 1 — Toolkit Hub Trial
  • Centralized access to available engineering tools.
  • Discipline-based navigation for Process and Piping.
  • Installable Toolkit Hub for mobile access.
  • Existing tools remain standalone applications during trial.
  • Proposal page introduced to collect new tool ideas.
